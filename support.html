Common setup steps

  1. Obtain a personal API key from Finnhub.
  2. Open Tickermon, enter the key, and add up to two US stock symbols.
  3. Add Tickermon from the Home Screen widget gallery. Widgets require iOS or iPadOS 17 or later.

Market-data account, plan, and API-key questions should be directed to Finnhub.
